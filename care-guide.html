BOTANICAL HEALTH GUIDE

Foliage Care Essentials

Master the art of keeping your tropical foliage lush, glossy, and thriving.

Light Balance

Filtered, bright indirect sunlight produces vigorous leaf splits and intense variegation without scorching sensitive foliage.

Hydration Routine

Allow top soil to dry moderately before watering. Never allow roots to sit in stagnant water.

Humidity & Aeration

Maintain ambient humidity above 60% with occasional foliage misting and adequate room airflow.
