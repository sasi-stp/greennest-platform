Plant care made simple

Care Guide

Give your foliage the right light, water, humidity and airflow — then let it settle and grow.

☼

Light

Bright, indirect light is a dependable starting point for most tropical foliage. Avoid sudden harsh afternoon sun.

◒

Water

Check the growing medium first. Water thoroughly when the upper layer has begun to dry.

◌

Humidity

Group tropical plants together and keep them away from very dry heat or strong drafts.

✦

Feeding

During active growth, use a balanced indoor-plant fertiliser according to its label directions.

Quick rule

Observe first. Water second.

Leaves, soil moisture, light and airflow tell you more than a fixed watering calendar. Check the plant before deciding what it needs.
