Foliage Care & Maintenance

Everything you need to know to keep your foliage thriving, vibrant, and lush.

Lighting Needs

Most foliage plants prefer medium to bright indirect sunlight. Avoid direct midday sun to prevent leaf scorching.

Watering Wisdom

Water when the top 2 inches of soil feel dry to touch. Always use pots with proper drainage holes to avoid root rot.

Humidity & Temp

Tropical foliage thrives in humidity above 50% and temperatures between 18°C to 28°C. Keep away from direct AC blasts.
